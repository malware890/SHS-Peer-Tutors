Missing a shift

Life happens — here's exactly what to do when you can't make your tutoring shift.

⚠

You're allowed up to 2 missed shifts per semester without a required makeup. Beyond that, you must complete makeup shifts to retain membership and service credit. More than 2 unexcused absences in a semester forfeits all credit for the semester.

📝

Step 1 — Submit the form

Fill out the Missing Shift Form at least 24 hours before your anticipated absence. This notifies the adult tutors in your content area.

📄

⏱

Emergency absence?

If something comes up with less than 24 hours' notice, still complete the form as soon as you can and contact your content area director directly so they can plan around it.

Making It Up

Two ways to make up a missed shift

📚

Content Database submission

Follow the submission instructions on the Content Database page. You can complete up to 2 makeup submissions per semester this way — additional submissions earn extra credit but don't count toward absence makeup.

🤝

In-person makeup shift

Coordinate directly with your Content Director to schedule a replacement shift. Unscheduled "makeup" sessions that weren't arranged in advance don't receive credit.
