Policies & Expectations

Peer Tutors Handbook

Everything you need to know about trainings, behavior, check-in, and what makes a great tutoring session.

📅

Attendance Policies

All mandatory general trainings require attendance — doors close at 3:45 PM.

  • Attend the Informational session, or complete its EdPuzzle, to qualify for membership
  • Attend the September & January Training sessions, or complete the EdPuzzle, to qualify for tutoring
  • You can miss only 1 of the 6 meetings all year without needing a makeup — a second miss earns a warning email from your director (sponsors CC'd)
  • Up to 2 missed shifts/semester without makeup work; beyond that, makeup shifts are required to retain membership and hours

Missing a shift? Fill out the form at least 24 hours in advance to notify adult tutors. Make it up via a Content Database submission or by emailing your Content Director.

🤝

Behavior Expectations

  • Tutoring time is for academic help — not socializing
  • No AirPods, headphones, or cell phones during shifts
  • Unsure of an answer? Ask another peer tutor first, then an adult tutor
  • Keep everything between you and your tutee confidential
  • Never share grades or test performance without the student's consent
✅

Check-In / Check-Out

  1. Arrive on time
  2. Check in at the Peer Tutors computer using your ID
  3. Put on a lanyard
  4. Confirm attendance with the adult tutor
  5. At the end: check out with the adult tutor, rescan your ID, return the lanyard
⭐

Model Tutor Session

  1. Introduce yourself and learn your tutee's name
  2. Clarify exactly what help they need
  3. Use questioning strategies rather than giving answers
  4. Let the tutee lead the conversation
  5. Build confidence with positive reinforcement
  6. Close by having them summarize what they learned
