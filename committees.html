Committees

Three committees work year-round on “improving the Stevenson tutoring experience and building community for both our club members and fellow patriots.” Sophomores and juniors can apply during fall recruitment.

📅

All committees meet in person, monthly — seven sessions across the 2026–2027 school year, October through April, Fridays from 3:30–5:00 PM.

🌐

Focuses on external engagement and building community beyond our own membership.
