What Does Tutoring Look Like?

One hour a week. A real difference.

Peer Tutors run weekly, one-hour volunteer shifts across multiple locations on campus, open to sophomores through seniors. Every session is built around social-emotional learning — not just getting the right answer, but building confidence along the way.

See Membership Requirements
  • Weekly one-hour shifts at convenient campus locations
  • Open to sophomores, juniors, and seniors
  • Built around social-emotional learning, not just answers
  • Builds confidence and communication skills for tutors
  • Strengthens subject mastery through teaching others
  • Creates real community between tutors and tutees
  • Looks great on college apps — and feels even better
