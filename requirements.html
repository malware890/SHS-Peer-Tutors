Eligibility

Peer Tutor membership requirements

General expectations apply to every tutor, plus subject-specific course requirements depending on your content area.

📅

Attendance Policy

  • Attend all General meetings
  • Tutor at least one hour weekly
  • Up to 2 missed shifts/semester without a required makeup
  • 3+ absences require makeup shifts to retain membership
  • 5+ consecutive absences results in removal
📝

Logging Volunteer Hours

Logged via InnerView at the end of each semester. Each tutoring shift and each General meeting counts as one hour. Your description should include:

  • Day & time of your tutoring shift
  • Subject tutored
  • Your tutoring experience, elaborated
  • Connections built within the community
  • Value & impact of the tutoring on you and your students
  • A picture of you tutoring
By Content Area

Subject-specific requirements

Mathematics

Enrollment in or completion of AP Precalculus BC, AP Calculus AB/BC, or AP Statistics.

Computer Science

All CS Peer Tutors must also meet the Math requirements above, and must be willing to tutor Math as well.

Science

Enrollment in or completion of any AP Science course or Earth Science.
